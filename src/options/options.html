Searching

Bookmarks show up once you type, below apps of the same relevance. They are read live while the popup is open — never stored, never sent anywhere. Turning this on asks for the bookmarks permission; turning it off hands the permission back.

Keeping in sync

Syncing adds apps that appeared and removes ones that are gone. An app is only removed after two syncs in a row have missed it, and a sync that suddenly cannot find a large part of your list is discarded rather than acted on — so a half-read page can never empty the launcher. Removal needs the portal tab to be the one on screen, which is what the visit sync is for; a sync of a background tab can only add.
